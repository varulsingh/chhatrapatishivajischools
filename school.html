About Our School

Welcome to Chhatrapati Shivaji Higher Secondary School

Established in 1994, Chhatrapati Shivaji Higher Secondary School is a premier educational institution under the "आकांक्षा शिक्षा एवं समाज कल्याण समिति" organization. We are committed to providing quality education that combines traditional values with modern teaching methodologies.

Our school is affiliated with the Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education and follows the CBSE curriculum. We offer a wide range of subjects for higher education including Arts, Mathematics, Science, Bio Science, and Commerce.

Under the guidance of our Director, Mr. B M Singh Gautam, and Principal, Mrs. Kalpana Singh, we strive to create an environment that nurtures intellectual curiosity, creativity, and social responsibility.
